## General feedback
In summary, the analysis of the augMENTOR profiles reveals distinct strengths and weaknesses across cognitive and 21st-century skills. The analysis of the performance data reveals that UPAT_A learners excel in cognitive and critical thinking skills and possess strong digital literacy and autonomy, yet they exhibit inconsistencies in creativity and potential gaps in communication and collaboration. Conversely, UPAT_B learners demonstrate strong creativity and collaboration skills but require development in autonomy and digital fluency. These findings highlight distinct educational needs aligned with each profile's characteristics.

The proposed policies address these needs by recommending targeted course designs and educator strategies grounded in the TESA framework. For UPAT_A, policies emphasize enhancing creativity through technology-rich, autonomous learning and collaborative digital projects. For UPAT_B, policies focus on scaffolded digital fluency development and fostering autonomy alongside collaborative learning. Educator-focused strategies include differentiated instruction, formative assessments, and metacognitive activities tailored to each profile. Collectively, these interventions are projected to improve learner engagement, promote balanced skill acquisition across cognitive and 21st-century competencies, and enhance overall educational outcomes within the course context.

## Discussion about learners' profiles
### augMENTOR profile: UPAT_A
**Characteristics:** Confident and highly autonomous individuals with strong competence and solid digital literacy, particularly skilled in digital teaching practices.

**Description:** The UPAT_A profile represents learners who exhibit high levels of autonomy, competence, and relatedness. A large proportion of this group reports “high” to “very high” autonomy, reflecting their independence and self-direction in both learning and teaching contexts. Competence is equally strong, with a significant number scoring in the top two tiers. Relatedness is also a key strength, suggesting strong social bonds and a collaborative mindset. In digital teaching skills, UPAT_A learners show strong agreement with their ability to implement classroom activities using Web 2.0 tools and digital platforms like Moodle or e-Class. They are confident in integrating TPACK principles (Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge) into their teaching strategies. Furthermore, they excel in supporting students' digital collaboration and learning, and in using digital environments for assessment. These learners are notably familiar with various digital learning tools tailored to their subject matter. Overall, UPAT_A profiles indicate tech-savvy educators or learners, with a progressive, independent approach to teaching and learning, coupled with robust self-belief in their capabilities.
### augMENTOR profile: UPAT_B
**Characteristics:** Supportive and socially aware learners who value inclusivity and collaboration, though showing room for growth in autonomy and digital fluency.

**Description:** The UPAT_B profile highlights individuals who report moderate levels of autonomy and competence, with relatedness being a strong point. This suggests that while they may not always take the lead, they thrive in collaborative environments and are deeply connected to their peers and learning communities. Their confidence in using digital tools for teaching is more varied. A larger percentage express disagreement or neutrality when asked about their ability to apply digital learning strategies or tools effectively. This may indicate a need for further support or training in integrating digital tools into pedagogical practice. Nonetheless, many still recognize the importance of technology and show some agreement in their ability to support students with digital tools. While they may not rate themselves as highly autonomous or digitally skilled as UPAT_A learners, UPAT_B individuals tend to be reflective, cooperative, and socially conscious, often aligning with values of inclusivity and group success.

(augMENTOR profile: UPAT_A)

Communication and Collaboration scores are not provided, but the profile is characterized by high autonomy and digital literacy.

  - Weaknesses: A significant outlier low score in Creativity suggests inconsistency in creative skill application; potential gaps in communication and collaboration skills are unknown due to missing data.

(augMENTOR profile: UPAT_B)

- **Key Observations**:
  - Strengths: Strong critical thinking, high creativity, strong collaboration and social awareness.

## Policy recommendations for supporting learners
#### #1 Supporting learners with confident and highly autonomous individuals with strong competence and solid digital literacy, particularly skilled in digital teaching practices.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be designed to leverage the high autonomy and digital literacy of UPAT_A learners while addressing inconsistencies in creativity and potential gaps in communication and collaboration skills. Emphasis should be placed on integrating technology-rich, autonomous learning activities that challenge creative thinking and foster peer interaction.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Enhanced Creative Problem-Solving Modules**: Courses should incorporate structured creative tasks using digital tools to stimulate consistent creativity, addressing the observed low creativity outlier (TESA Phase E: Activities for Implementation in the Classroom).
- **Collaborative Digital Projects**: Learning activities should include peer collaboration using digital platforms to develop communication and collaboration skills, complementing their autonomy (TESA Phase E).
- **Autonomy-Supportive Scaffolding**: Course design should provide scaffolding that supports self-directed learning while encouraging reflection on creative processes (TESA Phase F: Evaluation of the Educational Scenario).
- **Formative Creativity Assessments**: Implement ongoing formative assessments focused on creativity to monitor progress and provide feedback (TESA Phase F).
- **Integration of Emerging Technologies**: Use advanced digital tools aligned with the course content to deepen pedagogical and technological competence (TESA Phase A: The Teaching Object of the Educational Scenario).
#### #2 Supporting learners with supportive and socially aware learners who value inclusivity and collaboration, though showing room for growth in autonomy and digital fluency.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be structured to build autonomy and digital fluency in UPAT_B learners while capitalizing on their strengths in collaboration and creativity. Learning experiences should foster independent digital skills development alongside inclusive, socially aware group activities.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Digital Fluency Enhancement Workshops**: Courses should include targeted modules to improve digital literacy and autonomous use of educational technologies (TESA Phase A).
- **Collaborative Learning Frameworks**: Design activities that promote peer collaboration and social interaction to leverage their social awareness (TESA Phase E).
- **Autonomy Development Strategies**: Scaffolded tasks that gradually increase learner independence should be integrated to build self-directed learning skills (TESA Phase E).
- **Creativity Supportive Environments**: Provide diverse creative tasks with clear guidance to address occasional creativity challenges (TESA Phase E).
- **Formative Peer and Self-Assessments**: Incorporate assessments that encourage reflection on collaboration and autonomy progress (TESA Phase F).

## Policy recommendations for supporting educators and course providers
### #1 Supporting learners with confident and highly autonomous individuals with strong competence and solid digital literacy, particularly skilled in digital teaching practices.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Educators should be equipped to design and facilitate courses that harness the autonomy and digital literacy of UPAT_A learners while actively promoting creativity and collaborative skills. Strategies should include differentiated instruction, formative assessments, and technology integration to support these aims.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Implement Differentiated Creative Tasks Weekly**: Educators should assign creative problem-solving activities using digital tools on a weekly basis to address creativity inconsistencies (TESA Phase E).
- **Facilitate Structured Peer Collaboration Sessions**: Regularly scheduled collaborative projects should be facilitated to enhance communication and teamwork skills (TESA Phase E).
- **Use Formative Creativity Rubrics**: Educators should employ rubrics focused on creativity to provide timely feedback and guide learner reflection (TESA Phase F).
- **Incorporate Autonomy-Supportive Feedback**: Feedback should encourage self-regulation and independent learning strategies (TESA Phase F).
- **Integrate Emerging Technologies in Lessons**: Educators should consistently use relevant digital tools aligned with course objectives to deepen technological competence (TESA Phase A).
- **Monitor Learner Progress with Digital Analytics**: Use learning management system analytics to track engagement and skill development (TESA Phase F).
- **Provide Reflective Metacognitive Activities**: Educators should include activities that prompt learners to reflect on their creative and collaborative processes (TESA Phase E).
### #2 Supporting learners with supportive and socially aware learners who value inclusivity and collaboration, though showing room for growth in autonomy and digital fluency.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Educators should be supported to foster autonomy and digital fluency in UPAT_B learners through scaffolded instruction, collaborative learning facilitation, and targeted digital skills development. Emphasis should be placed on gradual independence and leveraging social strengths.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Design Scaffolded Digital Literacy Modules per Module**: Educators should implement stepwise digital skills training aligned with course content (TESA Phase A).
- **Organize Inclusive Group Work Weekly**: Facilitate group activities that promote social interaction and collaboration (TESA Phase E).
- **Apply Formative Self- and Peer-Assessment Tools**: Use assessments that encourage reflection on autonomy and collaboration progress (TESA Phase F).
- **Provide Incremental Autonomy Tasks**: Gradually increase task complexity and independence requirements (TESA Phase E).
- **Use Clear Rubrics for Creativity and Collaboration**: Educators should apply transparent criteria to guide learner efforts and feedback (TESA Phase F).
- **Incorporate Technology Use Demonstrations**: Regular demonstrations of digital tools should be provided to build fluency (TESA Phase A).
- **Facilitate Metacognitive Reflection Sessions**: Educators should schedule sessions for learners to reflect on their learning strategies and social interactions (TESA Phase E).

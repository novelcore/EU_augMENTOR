## General feedback
In summary, the analysis of the augMENTOR profiles reveals distinct strengths and weaknesses across cognitive and 21st-century skills. The analysis of the performance data reveals distinct learning needs across the three augMENTOR profiles. IASIS_A learners require targeted support in communication and creativity to complement their steady cognitive and collaboration skills. IASIS_B learners demonstrate strong overall skills but benefit from enhanced critical thinking challenges and social awareness activities. IASIS_C learners show high autonomy and collaboration strengths but need focused development in creativity and communication to fully leverage their leadership potential. These insights highlight the necessity for differentiated instructional strategies tailored to each profile's strengths and weaknesses.

The proposed policies, grounded in the TESA framework, address these needs by recommending learner-focused course designs that scaffold weaker skills while leveraging strengths, and educator-focused strategies that provide structured, formative, and technology-supported interventions. Implementation of these policies is projected to enhance learner engagement, foster skill acquisition in communication, creativity, critical thinking, and collaboration, and improve overall educational outcomes in professional social and cognitive competencies essential for mental health practitioners.

## Discussion about learners' profiles
### augMENTOR profile: IASIS_A
**Characteristics:** Solid interpersonal and problem-solving skills, grounded self-awareness, and steady but improvable academic performance.

**Description:** The IASIS_A profile reflects learners who demonstrate notable strengths in communication and problem-solving, with a reliable sense of autonomy and relatedness. Their communication skills and time management are considered solid, and are confident that they perform 'quite well' in these areas. A strong awareness and inclusion of minority groups in daily interactions is also a characteristic, suggesting a thoughtful and socially conscious disposition. Problem-solving skills are a clear asset, consistently rated positively. Autonomy and competence are present at medium to high levels, and relatedness-how connected they feel to others-is particularly strong, with most learners in this group scoring in the high category. However, academic indicators show moderate outcomes. Creativity and cognitive performance fall into the average range while collaboration is somewhat stronger, suggesting that learners with this profile work well in group contexts. Critical thinking scores are modest, and communication grades, while varied, tend to center a bit above average, indicating room for growth in formal expression despite strong self-perception.
### augMENTOR profile: IASIS_B
**Characteristics:** Well-rounded, confident learners who not only excel academically but also exhibit social awareness and a strong sense of belonging.

**Description:** The IASIS_B profile represents learners with a more robust and consistent performance across most domains. Self-assessments are generally confident, mostly considering communication and time management skills at the highest levels. Deep commitment to inclusivity is portrayed, leading in ratings for the inclusion of minority groups, and demonstrating excellent problem-solving capabilities. These learners also perceive the thematic content of their courses as highly helpful, suggesting they are both engaged and benefiting from the structure. Autonomy and competence levels are high, and demonstrate strong connections to their peers. Academically, learners that fall under this profile perform well, with creativity and cognitive skills both well above average. Their collaboration skills are solid, critical thinking is well-developed, and their communication grades align with their self-perception, also averaging well above average.
### augMENTOR profile: IASIS_C
**Characteristics:** High autonomy, intellectually capable individuals who thrive in collaborative and higher-order thinking tasks, positioning them as leaders in both academic and social-emotional domains.

**Description:** The IASIS_C profile corresponds to learners with high performance across several key indicators. Learners in this profile showcase strong creativity, with the high median scores, also excelling in critical thinking and communication-both in self-assessment and in graded performance. Autonomy is the most developed, with the greatest number reporting 'very high' levels, and their sense of competence and relatedness is equally strong. Cognitive performance is solid, averaging quite above average, and collaboration is particularly impressive. While time management and communication self-assessments are good, and problem-solving abilities are reliable, their inclusion of minority groups is moderate. Learners under this profile tend to find the thematic content helpful.

## Policy recommendations for supporting learners
#### #1 Supporting learners with solid interpersonal and problem-solving skills, grounded self-awareness, and steady but improvable academic performance.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be designed to scaffold communication and creativity skills for learners with characteristics similar to IASIS_A, while leveraging their strengths in collaboration and critical thinking. Emphasis should be placed on structured activities that promote expressive skills and creative problem-solving within professional contexts. The recommendations include targeted interventions to enhance communication confidence and creative engagement, aligned with the course's focus on social and professional skills.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Enhanced Communication Modules**: Structured asynchronous activities focusing on professional communication scenarios should be integrated, allowing learners to practice and receive feedback, addressing the Communication skill gap (TESA Phase E: Activities for Implementation in the Classroom).
- **Creativity Development Workshops**: Interactive e-activities promoting creative thinking through real-world problem-solving should be embedded, supporting the development of Creativity (TESA Phase E).
- **Collaborative Peer Learning**: Group assignments and peer feedback loops should be emphasized to leverage existing collaboration strengths and foster social learning (TESA Phase E).
- **Formative Assessments on Expressive Skills**: Frequent low-stakes assessments focusing on communication and creativity should be implemented to monitor progress and adapt learning paths (TESA Phase F: Evaluation of the Educational Scenario).
- **Use of Reflective e-Sheets**: Activities encouraging self-assessment and reflection on communication and creativity challenges should be included to promote metacognitive skills (TESA Phase E: Metacognitive Activities).
#### #2 Supporting learners with well-rounded, confident learners who not only excel academically but also exhibit social awareness and a strong sense of belonging.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should capitalize on the well-rounded and confident nature of IASIS_B learners by providing advanced challenges in critical thinking and problem-solving, while maintaining strong support for creativity and collaboration. The design should include complex scenarios that stimulate higher-order thinking and social awareness, aligned with the course's objectives of professional skill enhancement.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Advanced Critical Thinking Scenarios**: Incorporate case studies and decision-making exercises that require deep analysis and evaluation to strengthen critical thinking subskills (TESA Phase E).
- **Creativity and Innovation Labs**: Provide opportunities for creative expression through innovative tasks that connect theory with practice, enhancing creativity further (TESA Phase E).
- **Social Awareness and Inclusion Activities**: Design activities that promote understanding of diversity and inclusion, reinforcing social awareness (TESA Phase E).
- **Peer-led Discussions and Feedback**: Facilitate learner-led forums and peer review sessions to enhance communication and collaboration (TESA Phase E).
- **Continuous Formative Feedback**: Implement ongoing formative assessments to track learner confidence and skill development, enabling timely instructional adjustments (TESA Phase F).
#### #3 Supporting learners with high autonomy, intellectually capable individuals who thrive in collaborative and higher-order thinking tasks, positioning them as leaders in both academic and social-emotional domains.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be tailored to support the high autonomy and leadership potential of IASIS_C learners by providing opportunities for collaborative leadership and complex problem-solving, while addressing moderate creativity and communication skills. The course design should encourage self-directed learning and peer mentoring aligned with professional practice.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Leadership in Collaborative Projects**: Assign roles that require leading group activities to harness collaboration strengths and develop communication skills (TESA Phase E).
- **Complex Problem-Solving Tasks**: Integrate challenging scenarios that stimulate critical thinking and decision-making (TESA Phase E).
- **Creativity Enhancement Sessions**: Include targeted creative thinking exercises to boost moderate creativity scores (TESA Phase E).
- **Self-Directed Learning Modules**: Provide resources and activities that promote autonomy and self-regulation (TESA Phase E).
- **Reflective Practice and Peer Feedback**: Encourage reflective journaling and structured peer feedback to improve communication and metacognitive skills (TESA Phase E).

## Policy recommendations for supporting educators and course providers
### #1 Supporting learners with solid interpersonal and problem-solving skills, grounded self-awareness, and steady but improvable academic performance.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Educators should be equipped to scaffold communication and creativity development for IASIS_A learners by implementing structured, supportive, and interactive teaching strategies. Emphasis should be placed on frequent formative assessments, guided peer collaboration, and reflective practices to enhance learner engagement and skill acquisition.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Structured Communication Practice**: Educators should schedule weekly communication exercises with clear rubrics and feedback mechanisms (TESA Phase E).
- **Creativity-Focused Assignments**: Educators should introduce creativity tasks progressively, with scaffolding and exemplars (TESA Phase E).
- **Peer Collaboration Facilitation**: Educators should organize small group activities with rotating roles to build collaboration and social skills (TESA Phase E).
- **Formative Micro-Assessments**: Educators should implement short, frequent assessments to monitor communication and creativity progress (TESA Phase F).
- **Reflective e-Sheets Integration**: Educators should guide learners in completing reflective activities after each module to foster metacognition (TESA Phase E).
- **Use of Technology Tools**: Educators should incorporate communication platforms and creative software to support skill practice (TESA Phase D).
- **Personalized Feedback Delivery**: Educators should provide individualized feedback focusing on communication and creativity improvements (TESA Phase F).

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**

- **Creativity and Innovation Encouragement**: Educators should integrate creative problem-solving labs with real-world applications (TESA Phase E).
- **Inclusive Group Work Management**: Educators should ensure diverse group compositions to promote inclusion and social awareness (TESA Phase E).
- **Peer Feedback Systems**: Educators should establish structured peer review cycles to enhance communication and collaboration (TESA Phase E).
- **Ongoing Formative Assessment**: Educators should conduct regular formative assessments with detailed feedback (TESA Phase F).
- **Technology Integration**: Educators should utilize collaborative platforms and digital tools to support interactive learning (TESA Phase D).
- **Motivational Support and Coaching**: Educators should provide coaching sessions to sustain learner confidence and engagement (TESA Phase E).
### #3 Supporting learners with high autonomy, intellectually capable individuals who thrive in collaborative and higher-order thinking tasks, positioning them as leaders in both academic and social-emotional domains.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Educators should be guided to foster leadership and autonomy in IASIS_C learners by providing opportunities for self-directed learning, leadership roles in group tasks, and complex problem-solving challenges. Support should be given to enhance creativity and communication through reflective and peer mentoring activities.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**

- **Complex Scenario Design**: Educators should develop challenging problem-solving tasks requiring higher-order thinking (TESA Phase E).
- **Creativity Skill Workshops**: Educators should schedule targeted creativity enhancement sessions (TESA Phase E).

- **Reflective Practice Facilitation**: Educators should incorporate structured reflective journaling and peer feedback activities (TESA Phase E).
- **Use of Digital Collaboration Tools**: Educators should encourage use of technology to support communication and collaboration (TESA Phase D).

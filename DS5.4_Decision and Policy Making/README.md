# augMENTOR: Decision and Policy Making (DS5.4)

This folder contains the Policy Recommendations and Research Results generated by the augMENTOR AI-boosted solution. These documents translate technical algorithmic outcomes into actionable strategic guidance for educational stakeholders.
---

## Abstract

This component utilizes the knowledge extracted from the **Knowledge Graph (KG)** to generate high-level policy recommendations and strategic insights for educational stakeholders. It facilitates improved strategic planning, performance evaluation, and resource management by supplementing algorithmic capabilities with **pedagogically sound policy templates**.

---

## Folder Contents

### 1. Data and Templates

The files in this folder represent the final stage of the augMENTOR recommendation pipeline, formatted as human-readable policy briefs

---

## Purpose of these Reports

These documents are designed to support Evidence-based Policy Making (O4) by providing:


- **Differentiated Instructional Strategies**  
  Tailored course designs and learning pathways based on identified learner strengths, weaknesses, and profiles.

- **Educator Guidance**  
  Actionable recommendations to support teachers in scaffolding transversal skills such as creativity, critical thinking, collaboration, and self-regulation.

- **Strategic Planning**  
  High-level insights for course providers and decision-makers to improve learner engagement, optimize instructional design, and strengthen skill acquisition aligned with **21st-century competencies**.

---

## Technical Integration

These reports are grounded in the TESA framework and reflect data extracted from the augMENTOR Knowledge Graph. They serve as the primary dissemination tools for policy-makers and educational managers.

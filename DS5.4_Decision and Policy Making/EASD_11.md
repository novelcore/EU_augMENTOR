## General feedback
In summary, the analysis of the augMENTOR profiles reveals distinct strengths and weaknesses across cognitive and 21st-century skills. The analysis of the performance data reveals distinct learning needs for the two augMENTOR profiles within the context of the 'Karbonski otisak' course. EASD_A learners exhibit strong collaboration and communication skills but require targeted support to enhance their cognitive, creative, and critical thinking abilities, which are essential for understanding and addressing environmental challenges. Conversely, EASD_B learners demonstrate solid cognitive and creative skills but show weaknesses in collaboration, communication, and environmental engagement, which are critical for the course's social and affective learning goals.

The proposed policies address these needs by recommending differentiated course designs and educator strategies aligned with the TESA framework. For EASD_A, policies emphasize scaffolding cognitive tasks, fostering creativity through environmental projects, and leveraging their social strengths. For EASD_B, policies focus on embedding social-emotional learning, structured collaboration, and environmental reflection to build weaker skills. These targeted interventions are expected to enhance learner engagement, promote balanced skill development across cognitive and 21st-century competencies, and improve overall educational outcomes in future iterations of the course.

## Discussion about learners' profiles
### augMENTOR profile: EASD_A
**Characteristics:** Environmentally aware, socially conscious learners with solid collaboration and communication strengths, but average academic performance and room to grow in cognitive and creative domains.

**Description:** The EASD_A profile represents learners who are deeply concerned with environmental issues and exhibit a strong sense of ecological responsibility. They consistently agree with statements about the importance of climate change, personal responsibility, and environmental protection-indicating a clear alignment with sustainable values. This group is also motivated to take part in green initiatives and expresses confidence in their ability to contribute positively to the environment. Academically, learners in this profile perform moderately overall. Their collaboration skills stand out, suggesting they thrive in group settings and can work effectively with others. Communication scores are variable but lean above average, supported by high self-perception in this area. Creativity and cognitive performance, however, sit closer to the average range, with some variability across individuals. Critical thinking is modest and could be an area for targeted development. Overall, EASD_A learners are values-driven, socially aware, and excel in peer engagement, with the potential to strengthen their academic foundations-particularly in cognitive and creative disciplines.
### augMENTOR profile: EASD_B
**Characteristics:** Academically strong, confident learners with well-rounded cognitive and creative abilities, but lower levels of environmental concern and engagement.

**Description:** The EASD_B profile captures learners who perform strongly across academic domains. They demonstrate high scores in cognitive performance, creativity, and communication-suggesting both analytical strength and expressive capabilities. Collaboration and critical thinking are also well-developed, contributing to a balanced and effective academic presence. This group generally sees themselves as competent and confident, particularly in skills tied to academic success. Despite these strengths, their environmental attitudes show lower engagement. Compared to their peers in the EASD_A group, these learners express less concern about climate change, are less motivated to participate in eco-friendly activities, and show a more neutral stance on personal responsibility regarding sustainability. Understanding of environmental behaviors like carbon footprint and the effects of diet or transportation choices is also more limited. EASD_B learners shine in academic performance and cognitive abilities but may benefit from greater exposure to environmental education and sustainability-related themes to balance their intellectual growth with global awareness.

## Policy recommendations for supporting learners
#### #1 Supporting learners with environmentally aware, socially conscious learners with solid collaboration and communication strengths, but average academic performance and room to grow in cognitive and creative domains.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be designed to leverage the strong collaboration and communication skills of EASD_A learners while providing targeted support to enhance their cognitive, creative, and critical thinking abilities. Emphasis should be placed on integrating environmental awareness themes with active learning strategies that foster higher-order thinking and creativity.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Enhanced Cognitive Scaffolding**: Activities should be scaffolded to progressively build cognitive skills, incorporating problem-solving and analytical tasks aligned with the course’s focus on carbon footprint calculation and climate impact analysis (TESA Phase E: Activities for Implementation in the Classroom).
- **Creativity-Driven Projects**: Creative assignments such as designing sustainable solutions or environmental campaigns should be integrated to stimulate creativity and application of knowledge in real-world contexts (TESA Phase E).
- **Collaborative Environmental Problem-Solving**: Group projects should be structured to harness collaboration strengths while embedding critical thinking challenges related to environmental issues, promoting deeper engagement with course content (TESA Phase E).
- **Use of Reflective Metacognitive Activities**: Incorporate metacognitive exercises that encourage learners to reflect on their thinking processes and environmental attitudes, supporting affective and cognitive growth (TESA Phase E: Metacognitive Activities).
- **Digital Tools for Environmental Awareness**: Employ emerging technologies such as interactive simulations or carbon footprint calculators to enhance engagement and understanding of environmental concepts (TESA Phase D: Teaching Material of the Educational Scenario).
#### #2 Supporting learners with academically strong, confident learners with well-rounded cognitive and creative abilities, but lower levels of environmental concern and engagement.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be tailored to strengthen the collaboration, communication, and environmental engagement of EASD_B learners while maintaining their cognitive and creative strengths. Course design should incorporate social and environmental contexts that motivate these learners to connect academic content with real-world ecological concerns.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Social-Emotional Learning Integration**: Embed activities that develop empathy and environmental responsibility, such as community-based projects or peer discussions on climate impact, to enhance social consciousness (TESA Phase E: Activities for Implementation in the Classroom).
- **Collaborative Learning Structures**: Design structured peer collaboration opportunities with clear roles and communication goals to improve teamwork and communication skills (TESA Phase E).
- **Critical Thinking Enhancement through Environmental Contexts**: Use case studies and problem-based learning focused on environmental challenges to deepen critical thinking and engagement (TESA Phase E).
- **Creative Expression in Environmental Themes**: Encourage creative outputs like multimedia presentations or design thinking workshops that connect creativity with environmental topics (TESA Phase E).
- **Technology-Enhanced Interaction**: Utilize digital platforms that facilitate communication and collaboration among learners, fostering social skills and environmental dialogue (TESA Phase D and E).

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**

- **Facilitate Collaborative Group Work with Defined Roles**: Structure group activities with clear roles to maximize collaboration and communication effectiveness (TESA Phase E).
- **Incorporate Reflective Journals**: Encourage learners to maintain journals reflecting on their learning and environmental attitudes to foster metacognition (TESA Phase E: Metacognitive Activities).

- **Encourage Peer Feedback Sessions**: Organize structured peer review to enhance communication skills and collaborative learning (TESA Phase E).

- **Leverage Technology for Peer Interaction**: Use online forums and collaborative platforms to encourage communication and teamwork beyond the classroom (TESA Phase D and E).

- **Adjust Timing to Allow Social Skill Development**: Allocate sufficient time within lessons for social interaction and group discussions (TESA Phase E).

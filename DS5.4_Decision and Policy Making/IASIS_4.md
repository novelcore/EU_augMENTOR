## General feedback
In summary, the analysis of the augMENTOR profiles reveals distinct strengths and weaknesses across cognitive and 21st-century skills. The analysis of the performance data reveals distinct learning needs across the three augMENTOR profiles. IASIS_A learners require targeted support in communication and critical thinking to complement their interpersonal strengths and steady academic performance. IASIS_B learners, while well-rounded and confident, benefit from challenges that deepen creativity and cognitive skills to maximize their potential. IASIS_C learners demonstrate strong autonomy and leadership but need consistent reinforcement in communication and cognitive skills to ensure balanced development.

The proposed policies address these needs by recommending tailored course designs and educator strategies aligned with the TESA framework. Learner-focused policies emphasize scaffolded activities, formative assessments, and reflective practices to foster skill growth in contextually relevant ways. Educator-focused policies provide actionable strategies for structuring learning experiences, facilitating collaboration, and integrating technology to support diverse learner profiles. Collectively, these interventions are projected to enhance learner engagement, promote comprehensive skill acquisition, and improve overall educational outcomes in future courses for mental health professionals.

## Discussion about learners' profiles
### augMENTOR profile: IASIS_A
**Characteristics:** Solid interpersonal and problem-solving skills, grounded self-awareness, and steady but improvable academic performance.

**Description:** The IASIS_A profile reflects learners who demonstrate notable strengths in communication and problem-solving, with a reliable sense of autonomy and relatedness. Their communication skills and time management are considered solid, and are confident that they perform 'quite well' in these areas. A strong awareness and inclusion of minority groups in daily interactions is also a characteristic, suggesting a thoughtful and socially conscious disposition. Problem-solving skills are a clear asset, consistently rated positively. Autonomy and competence are present at medium to high levels, and relatedness-how connected they feel to others-is particularly strong, with most learners in this group scoring in the high category. However, academic indicators show moderate outcomes. Creativity and cognitive performance fall into the average range while collaboration is somewhat stronger, suggesting that learners with this profile work well in group contexts. Critical thinking scores are modest, and communication grades, while varied, tend to center a bit above average, indicating room for growth in formal expression despite strong self-perception.
### augMENTOR profile: IASIS_B
**Characteristics:** Well-rounded, confident learners who not only excel academically but also exhibit social awareness and a strong sense of belonging.

**Description:** The IASIS_B profile represents learners with a more robust and consistent performance across most domains. Self-assessments are generally confident, mostly considering communication and time management skills at the highest levels. Deep commitment to inclusivity is portrayed, leading in ratings for the inclusion of minority groups, and demonstrating excellent problem-solving capabilities. These learners also perceive the thematic content of their courses as highly helpful, suggesting they are both engaged and benefiting from the structure. Autonomy and competence levels are high, and demonstrate strong connections to their peers. Academically, learners that fall under this profile perform well, with creativity and cognitive skills both well above average. Their collaboration skills are solid, critical thinking is well-developed, and their communication grades align with their self-perception, also averaging well above average.
### augMENTOR profile: IASIS_C
**Characteristics:** High autonomy, intellectually capable individuals who thrive in collaborative and higher-order thinking tasks, positioning them as leaders in both academic and social-emotional domains.

**Description:** The IASIS_C profile corresponds to learners with high performance across several key indicators. Learners in this profile showcase strong creativity, with the high median scores, also excelling in critical thinking and communication-both in self-assessment and in graded performance. Autonomy is the most developed, with the greatest number reporting 'very high' levels, and their sense of competence and relatedness is equally strong. Cognitive performance is solid, averaging quite above average, and collaboration is particularly impressive. While time management and communication self-assessments are good, and problem-solving abilities are reliable, their inclusion of minority groups is moderate. Learners under this profile tend to find the thematic content helpful.

## Policy recommendations for supporting learners
#### #1 Supporting learners with solid interpersonal and problem-solving skills, grounded self-awareness, and steady but improvable academic performance.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be designed to scaffold communication and critical thinking skills for learners with solid interpersonal abilities but moderate academic performance, such as IASIS_A. Emphasis should be placed on structured collaborative activities, guided reflection, and practical communication exercises to enhance these weaker areas while leveraging their interpersonal strengths.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Enhanced Communication Modules**: Courses should integrate targeted communication skill-building exercises, including role-plays and scenario-based discussions, to improve professional communication (TESA Phase E: Activities for Implementation in the Classroom).
- **Critical Thinking Scaffolding**: Structured problem-solving tasks with stepwise guidance should be incorporated to develop critical thinking progressively (TESA Phase E).
- **Collaborative Learning Emphasis**: Group activities should be designed to capitalize on interpersonal strengths, promoting peer learning and social engagement (TESA Phase E).
- **Formative Feedback Integration**: Frequent formative assessments with personalized feedback should be embedded to monitor and support skill development (TESA Phase F: Evaluation of the Educational Scenario).
- **Use of Reflective e-Activities**: Incorporation of metacognitive activities encouraging self-assessment and reflection to foster self-awareness and continuous improvement (TESA Phase E).
#### #2 Supporting learners with well-rounded, confident learners who not only excel academically but also exhibit social awareness and a strong sense of belonging.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be structured to challenge and extend the well-rounded and confident IASIS_B learners by incorporating advanced creativity and cognitive tasks, while maintaining strong collaborative and communication opportunities. This will support their social awareness and sense of belonging while pushing their innovative and analytical capacities.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Advanced Creative Problem-Solving Tasks**: Courses should include open-ended projects and innovation challenges to stimulate creativity (TESA Phase E).
- **Cognitive Deepening Activities**: Integration of complex case studies and decision-making scenarios to enhance cognitive engagement (TESA Phase E).
- **Peer-Led Collaborative Projects**: Opportunities for IASIS_B learners to lead group work, reinforcing leadership and social skills (TESA Phase E).
- **Technology-Enhanced Learning Tools**: Use of digital platforms for interactive simulations and collaborative tools to enrich learning experiences (TESA Phase D: Teaching Material).
- **Continuous Formative Assessment**: Implement micro-assessments to provide ongoing feedback and encourage self-regulation (TESA Phase F).
#### #3 Supporting learners with high autonomy, intellectually capable individuals who thrive in collaborative and higher-order thinking tasks, positioning them as leaders in both academic and social-emotional domains.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Future courses should be tailored to support the high autonomy and leadership qualities of IASIS_C learners by providing flexible, self-directed learning paths that emphasize communication skill enhancement and cognitive consistency. Collaborative leadership roles and critical reflection should be encouraged to maximize their potential.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Self-Directed Learning Opportunities**: Courses should offer modular content allowing learners to choose focus areas, fostering autonomy (TESA Phase E).
- **Communication Skill Workshops**: Targeted activities to strengthen professional communication, including feedback on presentations and written communication (TESA Phase E).
- **Leadership in Collaborative Tasks**: Structured roles in group activities to develop leadership and peer mentoring (TESA Phase E).
- **Cognitive Skill Reinforcement**: Use of scaffolded cognitive exercises to address variability and ensure consistent performance (TESA Phase E).
- **Reflective Practice Integration**: Encourage critical self-assessment and peer feedback to deepen metacognitive skills (TESA Phase E).

## Policy recommendations for supporting educators and course providers
### #1 Supporting learners with solid interpersonal and problem-solving skills, grounded self-awareness, and steady but improvable academic performance.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Educators should be equipped to support IASIS_A learners by implementing structured, scaffolded learning experiences that enhance communication and critical thinking while leveraging interpersonal strengths. Strategies should include frequent formative assessments and reflective activities to monitor progress.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Scaffolded Communication Exercises**: Educators should introduce incremental communication tasks weekly, such as role-plays and scenario discussions, to build confidence and skill (TESA Phase E).
- **Guided Critical Thinking Tasks**: Weekly problem-solving sessions with stepwise guidance and feedback should be provided to develop analytical skills (TESA Phase E).
- **Facilitation of Collaborative Groups**: Educators should organize small peer groups to foster interpersonal engagement and peer support (TESA Phase E).
- **Formative Micro-Assessments**: Short quizzes and reflective journals should be used regularly to provide timely feedback (TESA Phase F).
- **Use of Reflective e-Sheets**: Educators should assign metacognitive activities after each module to encourage self-assessment (TESA Phase E).
- **Personalized Feedback Sessions**: Scheduled one-on-one feedback opportunities should be offered to address individual challenges (TESA Phase F).
- **Integration of Technology Tools**: Educators should utilize communication platforms and collaborative tools to enhance interaction and engagement (TESA Phase D).
### #2 Supporting learners with well-rounded, confident learners who not only excel academically but also exhibit social awareness and a strong sense of belonging.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Educators should be prepared to challenge IASIS_B learners by incorporating advanced creative and cognitive tasks while supporting leadership in collaborative settings. Regular formative assessments and technology integration should be emphasized to sustain engagement and skill growth.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Design of Complex Creative Projects**: Educators should assign open-ended projects biweekly to stimulate creativity and innovation (TESA Phase E).
- **Facilitation of Leadership Roles**: Educators should rotate leadership roles in group activities to develop social and organizational skills (TESA Phase E).
- **Use of Digital Collaborative Platforms**: Weekly use of technology tools for group work and simulations should be encouraged (TESA Phase D and E).
- **Implementation of Formative Micro-Assessments**: Frequent short assessments should be used to monitor understanding and provide feedback (TESA Phase F).
- **Encouragement of Peer Teaching**: Educators should organize peer-led sessions to reinforce knowledge and social belonging (TESA Phase E).
- **Integration of Reflective Practices**: Educators should prompt learners to engage in self and peer reflection after major tasks (TESA Phase E).
- **Provision of Challenging Cognitive Tasks**: Educators should include case studies and decision-making exercises to deepen cognitive skills (TESA Phase E).
### #3 Supporting learners with high autonomy, intellectually capable individuals who thrive in collaborative and higher-order thinking tasks, positioning them as leaders in both academic and social-emotional domains.
🟢 Policy recommendation: Educators should be guided to support IASIS_C learners by providing flexible, autonomous learning paths with targeted communication skill development and leadership opportunities. Consistent formative feedback and reflective activities should be integrated to ensure balanced skill growth.

**Guidelines on the recommendation:**
- **Facilitation of Self-Directed Learning**: Educators should provide modular content and allow learners to select focus areas weekly (TESA Phase E).
- **Targeted Communication Workshops**: Educators should schedule focused sessions on professional communication skills, including presentations and writing (TESA Phase E).
- **Assignment of Leadership Roles in Groups**: Educators should assign leadership and mentoring roles in collaborative tasks regularly (TESA Phase E).
- **Regular Scaffolded Cognitive Exercises**: Educators should implement weekly cognitive tasks with progressive difficulty to ensure consistency (TESA Phase E).
- **Use of Reflective e-Activities**: Educators should encourage critical self-assessment and peer feedback after each module (TESA Phase E).
- **Provision of Personalized Feedback**: Scheduled individual feedback sessions should be conducted to address specific learner needs (TESA Phase F).
- **Integration of Collaborative Technology Tools**: Educators should utilize digital platforms to facilitate communication and collaboration (TESA Phase D and E).
